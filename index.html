04 / THE PHYSICAL FORGE

Keep building online.
Come together in person.

The foundry is a boundaryless workspace. Code, conversations, and shared intent keep the work moving every day. When a problem needs everyone around the same table, take it into the room. Build trust, work intensely, then bring what you learn back here.

01 / SHOW THE WORK

Demo days.

Bring what you shipped online to a live audience. Show it working, get direct feedback, and meet the people who can help with the next version.

02 / BUILD AT FULL SPEED

Hackathons.

A focused sprint in the same place. Form a small team, choose a hard problem, and take it from an idea to something you can run.

03 / THINK OUT LOUD

Whiteboard sessions.

Pull apart a Rust system design or an AI audio route. Draw, challenge assumptions, and leave with an approach everyone understands.

Start with a shared room and a clear purpose. Add a place and time to your session plan, meet in person, and keep the code and conversation here.
